## Coquette Furniture 1.0.0 "Sweet Home"

The first release.

- **37 pieces of furniture in six colourways** (Baby Pink, White, Cream, Lavender, Mint and Baby Blue) for the bedroom, living room, kitchen, bathroom and garden, plus **4 potted plants** and **13 decorations**: 239 blocks.
- **Working furniture**: sit on sofas, chairs, benches, pillows, swings and bathtubs; sleep in the canopy bed; storage from 5 to 54 slots with doors and drawers that open; a stove that cooks like a smoker; endless sink water; bubble baths; strawberry tea; a pampering vanity; candles, lamps, music boxes and a birdhouse.
- **Joining pieces**: sofas, benches, dining tables, rugs and picnic blankets join with their neighbours.
- **Decorations sink** onto tables, shelves, sofas, beds and slabs.
- **Interior Designer Mode**: the Interior Design Catalog (press K) with search, colour filters, 3D previews, live recipes and creative delivery.
- **Five furniture sets**: Princess Bedroom, Strawberry Cottage, Lavender Dreams, Angelcore Room and Pastel Kitchen.
- **Recolouring** with dyes, in the world and in the crafting grid.
- A commented config (`coquettefurniture-common.toml`, `coquettefurniture-client.toml`).

- **Multiloader**: one release for Forge and Fabric on 1.20.1, and NeoForge and Fabric on 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3.

| Minecraft | Loaders | Also needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1.20.1 | Forge 47+, Fabric (Loader 0.15+, Fabric API) | Nothing on Forge |
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1+, Fabric (Fabric API) | Nothing on NeoForge |
| 26.2 | NeoForge 26.2, Fabric (Fabric API) | Nothing on NeoForge |
| 26.3 | NeoForge 26.3, Fabric (Fabric API) | Nothing on NeoForge |

Install on both the server and every client.
